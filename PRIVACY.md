# Privacy Policy — Highlights

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

Highlights does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## What the app records

Highlights records video and audio from your device's camera and microphone so it can save the
clips you mark. That footage is written to the app's own storage on your device.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no third-party SDK of any kind. The app
makes no network connections. Recorded footage never leaves your device unless you explicitly save
a clip to your photo library, at which point it is handled by iOS as any other video would be.

## What is stored on your device

- **Recorded footage**, held temporarily while you record. Footage older than the retention window
  you choose in Settings is deleted automatically, except for segments a clip you marked still
  needs.
- **Clips you marked**, along with any trim and framing you applied.
- **Your settings**, such as retention window, clip length, and export resolution.

You can delete any clip from the Clips list, and remove all stored footage at any time from
Settings → Storage → Delete all stored footage. Deleting the app removes everything it holds.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — to record video. Required.
- **Microphone** — to record sound alongside the video. Required.
- **Photo library (add only)** — to save the clips you export. The app can add photos and videos
  but cannot read your library.

## Children

Highlights collects no data from anyone, of any age.
