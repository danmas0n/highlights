## Description (4000)

You're on the sideline. Your kid does something great. By the time you get your phone up, it's
over.

Highlights fixes that. Put your phone on a tripod, press record, and forget about it. When
something good happens, tap the screen — the previous 25 seconds, plus a few after, become a clip.
No filming the whole game. No scrubbing through ninety minutes on Sunday night to find the two
moments that mattered.

HOW IT WORKS
• The camera records continuously into a rolling buffer, keeping only the recent past
• Tap anywhere on the screen to mark a moment. A flash and a buzz confirm it
• The last 25 seconds plus 8 after the tap are saved as a clip. Tap late and it's still there
• Everything else is discarded as you go, so a whole game fits comfortably on your phone

FRAME LATER, NOT DURING
It records in 4K so you can crop in afterwards with no loss of quality. On the sideline, keep your
player somewhere in frame; at home, drag a box around the action and save. A 2× crop of 4K is
exactly 1080p — nothing is upscaled. Your phone's real lenses are there when the field is far away.

EDIT ON THE SPOT
• Trim with drag handles, like Photos
• Frame the crop, or let auto-follow track your player
• Full-screen review to see whether it was actually any good
• Save to your camera roll and send it to the grandparents

BUILT FOR A REAL SIDELINE
• Halftime? Stop and restart. Every clip stays intact
• Dim the screen to save battery while still recording and still accepting taps
• Marks survive the app being closed, so a hot phone doesn't cost you the first half
• A Bluetooth camera clicker works if you'd rather not touch the phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There's no account, no server, and no analytics. Footage never leaves your phone unless you save
a clip to Photos. The source code is public.

I built this for my own son's high school and club games. If you've got a kid who plays anything,
I hope it's useful to you too. It's free. There's a tip jar if it earns you a highlight you'd have
missed — it unlocks nothing.

## Notes for App Review

This is a camera app whose main screen is a live viewfinder, so it can look like it "does
nothing" at first glance. The first launch shows a four-screen tour explaining the flow. To
exercise it:

1. Allow camera and microphone access when prompted.
2. Point the phone at anything and press the red record button at the bottom.
3. Wait about 20 seconds, then tap anywhere on the screen. A white flash and a "Marked" banner
   confirm the moment was saved.
4. Press the record button again to stop.
5. Tap "Clips" (bottom left). The marked moment is listed. Open it to play, trim, and frame it;
   "Save to Photos" exports it to the camera roll.

The tip jar (Settings → About) contains three consumable in-app purchases. They unlock nothing —
every feature is available without purchase.

No account is required and the app makes no network connections. It must be tested on a physical
device; the Simulator has no camera and the app says so.
